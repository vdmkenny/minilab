minilab

Scan, develop and export film, a roll at a time.

Colour and B&W negatives and slides · macOS, Linux and Windows · Free and open source

Download Source on GitHub

Scan on a flatbed, photograph your negatives with a camera, or bring in finished scans from a lab. minilab finds the frames, inverts the negatives, corrects the colour and exports the results.

Raw

minilab switches the scanner's own processing off and scans raw: linear, 16 bits per channel, with the orange mask kept. The Epson V200 has a measured profile. For other SANE scanners, minilab works the raw settings out from the driver's options.

Camera RAW files work the same way. Develop measures the film base in the gaps between frames and inverts in density.

Frame detection

minilab finds the frames on the scanner's preview, on a strip scan or on a camera shot, for 35mm, half frame, 120 and the other formats.

Fix any box by hand, then scan the frames into the roll. A strip scan you import becomes one frame for each frame on it.

A roll at a time

A roll holds its scans, its film, camera and notes. Scan it, develop it, export it.

Settings sync across the roll. Exports are TIFF or JPEG in sRGB, Display P3, Adobe RGB or ProPhoto RGB, with the roll's camera, lens, film stock, date and your own fields as EXIF and XMP.

Colour and looks

For stocks with a published datasheet, minilab uses the maker's curves to open up the shadows, and it can balance the colour from the stock's data.

On top goes a look: Frontier emulation, 85 film looks from three free collections, any .cube LUT, or a fitted look that matches your lab, made from a few frames scanned by both.

Also

  • Formats 35mm, half frame, 120, 127, 110, 126, APS, Minox, sheet film and glass plates
  • Lab scans kept as they are, next to your other rolls
  • Flat field evens out the light behind camera scans
  • Ratings stars and rejects, with Lightroom's keys
  • Loupe the scan at full resolution, to check focus and grain
  • Command line import, scan and develop from the terminal

    macOS

    The builds aren't signed, so run this once in the unpacked folder to let minilab and its scanner drivers through:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine .

    Linux and Windows

    To scan on Linux, install your distribution's SANE package. On Windows, install your scanner maker's TWAIN driver.

    To build minilab yourself, see the README.
